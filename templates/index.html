Why EcoLoop AI?

Most campuses and small offices already produce sustainability data. It sits in notebooks, WhatsApp groups and spreadsheets, so nobody can act on it. EcoLoop collects it in one place and turns it into next steps.

Waste analysis

Log waste by photo or category, get a material classification, and see how much of it could have been diverted from landfill.

Carbon accounting

Estimate monthly emissions from electricity, travel, food and waste using emission factors you can edit for your own region.

Smart recycling

Track every handover by material and method, and watch your recycling rate against goals you set yourself.

Zero-waste planning

Turn intentions into measurable goals with deadlines, progress and a status that updates itself.

Biodiversity tracking

Record the birds, trees and insects around you to build a local baseline that shows change over time.

Sustainability reports

Generate a monthly report with waste, carbon, recycling and biodiversity figures, ready to print or download.

How it works

Four steps, repeated as often as you like. The more you log, the sharper the recommendations become.

1

Enter or upload data

Photograph a waste item or type in a quantity. Add recycling handovers, observations and a monthly carbon assessment.

2

EcoLoop analyses it

The classifier labels the material and the carbon engine converts your inputs into estimated emissions.

3

Get recommendations

Rules run over your own numbers and return prioritised actions with an estimated benefit for each one.

4

Track your impact

Watch your sustainability score, recycling rate and footprint move month by month, and export a report.
